## What is Word Master Pro?

Word Master Pro is an Android vocabulary learning app that captures words from your clipboard and turns them into flashcards with spaced repetition scheduling.

## Key Features

- **Clipboard analysis** — Automatically detects and captures new words from your clipboard
- **Spaced repetition** — Optimized review scheduling for long-term retention
- **Word details** — Definitions, example sentences, and pronunciation
- **Progress tracking** — Visual stats for your vocabulary growth

## Why I Built This

Learning vocabulary is most effective when it comes from context you actually encounter. Instead of memorizing word lists, Word Master Pro captures the words you naturally come across and helps you retain them.
