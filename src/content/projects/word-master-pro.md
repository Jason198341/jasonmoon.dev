## What It Does

Word Master Pro captures words from your clipboard and turns them into flashcards with spaced repetition. Learn vocabulary from the content you actually read, not random word lists.

## Key Features

- **Clipboard capture** — Automatically detects new words from your clipboard
- **Spaced repetition** — Optimized review scheduling for long-term retention
- **Word details** — Definitions, example sentences, pronunciation
- **Progress tracking** — Visual stats for vocabulary growth
