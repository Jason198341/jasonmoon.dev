## The Backstory

Our family has always loved reading — but everyone reads at their own pace, in their own corner. My daughter finishes a book a week, my son takes a month. I wanted to turn that into something fun — a friendly competition where we could see who's ahead, cheer each other on, and share what we've learned.

So I built a reading race. Every family member tracks their books, and a visual race track shows who's leading each month. But it's not just about speed — we share reviews, recommend books to each other, and collect reading tips that make everyone a better reader.

## Key Features

- **Monthly Race Track** — Visual progress bars showing each family member's reading pace with animated race positions
- **Leaderboard** — Real-time rankings with streak tracking and monthly awards
- **Book Reviews** — Share thoughts on finished books with ratings, favorite quotes, and recommendations
- **Reading Tips** — Curated collection of reading strategies that family members discover and share
- **Supabase Realtime** — Live updates so everyone sees progress as it happens
- **Family Dashboard** — At-a-glance view of total books read, active streaks, and monthly goals

## Why I Built This

Reading alone is good. Reading together as a family — and making it a game — is transformative. This platform turned our scattered reading habits into a shared adventure where everyone motivates each other.
