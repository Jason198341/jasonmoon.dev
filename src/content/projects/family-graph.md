## The Backstory

Our family has always loved reading — but everyone reads at their own pace, in their own corner. I wanted to turn that into something shared: not a competition, but a journey we take together. A place where we track our reading, cheer each other on, share thoughts about books, and even write letters to each other about what we've read.

This started as a simple reading tracker — but after consulting with psychologists, education experts, and UX researchers, it evolved into something much deeper: a cooperative family reading ecosystem designed to nurture healthy habits without the stress of competition.

## Key Features

- **Reading Journey Dashboard** — Visual progress tracks for each family member showing monthly reading pace, with cooperative (non-competitive) presentation
- **Reading Radar Analysis** — 4-dimension SVG radar chart per person measuring Volume, Quality, Sharing, and Diversity of reading
- **AI Reading Coach "Chaekbeot"** — AI-powered coach (Fireworks deepseek-v3p1) that understands your family context and provides personalized reading strategies, book recommendations, and weekly missions
- **Book Reviews & Recommendations** — Share thoughts with star ratings, recommend books to family, and browse a cross-family community board
- **Reading Letters** — Send heartfelt letters about books to family members, building emotional connection through reading
- **Daily Highlight Quotes** — Record memorable passages from books and build a family collection of "명문장" (beautiful sentences)
- **Cooperative Achievement System** — Family tree that grows from seed to mighty tree as you read together (15 badges including streaks, milestones, and collaborative goals)
- **Cross-Family Community** — See what other families are reading, discover books through community reviews, and compare reading patterns
- **Accessibility** — Font size toggle (Normal/Large/XL), minimum 12px fonts throughout, responsive design
- **Supabase Realtime** — Live updates with Google OAuth, family creation/invitation system, and row-level security

## Design Philosophy

After a simulated expert panel (psychologists, reading specialists, UX designers, gamification experts, and family users), we made deliberate choices:

1. **Cooperation over competition** — No rankings or sorting. Members appear in family order, not by performance
2. **"Reading King" not "MVP"** — Warm, encouraging language instead of corporate/competitive terminology
3. **Growth metaphor** — The family tree grows from a seed to a mighty tree, representing collective achievement
4. **Multi-dimensional assessment** — The radar chart prevents single-metric obsession by measuring 4 aspects of reading health
5. **Emotional connection** — Reading letters and highlight quotes create meaningful family bonds beyond just tracking numbers

## Why I Built This

Reading alone is good. Reading together as a family — and making it a shared adventure — is transformative. This platform turned our scattered reading habits into a connected journey where everyone motivates each other, not through pressure, but through warmth and shared discovery.
