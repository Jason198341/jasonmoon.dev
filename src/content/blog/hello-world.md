## A Brief Introduction

I'm Jason Moon — an automotive engineer by day, a systems designer by night.

For the past year, I've been building web apps, AI-powered tools, and multi-agent systems during evenings and weekends. Each project lives on my [projects page](/projects), but projects alone don't capture the _thinking_ behind them.

## Why a Blog?

Three reasons:

1. **Process over product.** A finished project shows the outcome, not the dozens of design decisions, dead ends, and trade-offs that got me there. Writing forces me to articulate those.

2. **Learning in public.** I learn faster when I explain things. If something I write helps someone else avoid a pitfall, that's a bonus.

3. **A home base.** Social media posts disappear. Blog posts on my own domain don't. This is the long-term archive.

## What to Expect

Posts will cover:

- **Dev** — practical notes on React, Astro, Supabase, deployment
- **AI** — prompt engineering, LLM integration, agent architectures
- **Tutorials** — step-by-step guides from my projects
- **Essays** — broader reflections on building, learning, and working

No fixed schedule. Quality over quantity.

## Let's Go

If you want to follow along, check back here or reach out on the [contact page](/contact). Every post starts from something I genuinely needed to figure out.
