## What Happened

Anthropic officially launched the **Claude Enterprise Plugin Marketplace** on February 25, 2026 — less than a month after introducing the first plugins on January 30. The marketplace includes department-specific plugins (design, HR, asset management), a custom plugin development toolkit, and a distribution channel for enterprise customers.

This is the clearest signal yet that Anthropic is building a **platform ecosystem**, not just a chatbot. The progression from MCP servers → plugins → marketplace mirrors the classic platform playbook that made iOS App Store and Salesforce AppExchange dominant in their categories.

## Why This Matters

### The MCP → Plugin → Marketplace Pipeline

For developers who've been building MCP servers, this is a direct monetization path. MCP servers were the technical foundation — standardized tool interfaces that any AI model could call. Plugins wrapped those interfaces in discoverable, installable packages. The marketplace now adds distribution and potentially revenue sharing.

If you've been building MCP integrations, you're already halfway to having a product on this marketplace.

### Enterprise Lock-In Through Customization

Department-specific plugins create deep organizational dependencies. Once a finance team builds workflows around a Claude plugin that connects to their ERP system, switching costs become enormous. This is Anthropic's enterprise moat strategy — not just better AI, but irreplaceable workflows.

### The Developer Opportunity

Third-party plugin development for Claude is now a viable business model. The combination of enterprise distribution (companies already paying $100-200/month per seat) and department-level customization creates genuine demand for specialized tools.

## What You Can Do

1. **If you have MCP servers**: Package them as plugins. The marketplace gives you instant distribution to Claude's enterprise customer base.
2. **Evaluate the plugin development toolkit**: Anthropic's custom dev tools likely include testing harnesses, schema validation, and deployment automation that reduce the friction of plugin creation.
3. **Identify domain-specific gaps**: The highest-value plugins will be those solving industry-specific problems (healthcare compliance, legal document review, financial reporting) that general-purpose AI can't handle well.
